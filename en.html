Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This policy applies to the Evrio Android app provided by albay19. Contact us about privacy or support at: uluzman47@gmail.com

Information processed on your device

Selected PDFs and photos, document names, folders, favourites, recognized text, saved signatures, editing and scan drafts, dates, reminders, checklists and preparations stay on your device. Document processing and text recognition run locally. Evrio does not automatically send this data to a developer server. There are no accounts, advertising SDKs, usage analytics or automatic crash reports.

Permissions

The system camera opens when you choose to scan or photograph a signature. Only files you select or share with Evrio are imported. Notification permission supports reminders; lock-screen messages do not reveal document names or contents. Android handles app-lock authentication; Evrio receives no fingerprint or face data.

Sharing and backups

You choose export, sharing and backup destinations. Copies sent to other apps or cloud services are governed by those services. There is no automatic cloud sync, and Android system backup is disabled. Optional encrypted backups use a password-derived key and AES-GCM-256. Backup passwords are not saved or recoverable. An unencrypted ZIP exposes its contents and saved signature to whoever can open it. The screen lock does not add encryption to archive files.

Retention and deletion

The archive remains on your device until you delete it or clear app data. Documents first move to Trash; permanent deletion removes the local archive record. Delete exported copies and backups separately. Drafts are excluded from archive backups; saving or explicitly discarding removes the corresponding draft. Temporary sharing files older than one day are removed on the next launch. Uninstalling the app may erase local data.

Support correspondence

A support note contains only the app version, platform, language and your description; it is not sent automatically. If you email us, your address, message and chosen attachments are used to respond to your request. Correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Do not attach personal documents that are unnecessary for support.

Your rights and changes

You can view, export and delete local data in the app. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information in support correspondence; your applicable legal rights remain available. When this policy changes, we update this page and its date.
